🎉 I’m excited to share what I’ve been building — **PlayPicks**, an AI-powered assistant that helps sports fans stay informed and make smarter decisions with powerful stat-based insights.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;For a long time, diving deeper into sports analysis meant:
- Scouring multiple websites full of complex stats  
- Relying on hearsay from forums and Discords  
- Digging through a firehose of info just to spot trends  

💡 **PlayPicks changes that** — powered by GPT-4o + RAG, it helps users:
- Ask sports-related questions and get fast, relevant breakdowns  
- Upload player stats or game slips for contextual insights  
- Stay updated with curated weekly newsletters tailored to their favorite sport  

🧠 **What’s next?**  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I’m working on bringing PlayPicks to the iOS App Store, with a mobile app that gives you the same power on the go. More updates on that soon.

🛠️ **Still building. Always improving.**  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I’d love your feedback. Whether you're into sports or AI, I’d be excited to hear your thoughts. If you run into bugs, have ideas, or just want to share your experience, feel free to reach out to me directly at `support@play-picks.com` — I’ll personally take a look.

👉 **Check it out** @ [play-picks.com](https://play-picks.com)
